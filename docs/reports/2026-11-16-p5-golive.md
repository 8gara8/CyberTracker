## What the mornings showed

These are inputs for calibration (SPEC §10, Calibration follow-up). None of them needed a fix before the next
morning.

1. **First drafts.** On 14 Nov the first attempt still failed after two repair rounds (TOP3_WHY and
   WORDS_OVER, with a SOURCE_ORPHAN warning), and the retry passed. The other three mornings passed on the
   first attempt with one repair round.
2. **`generated_at` is the agent's guess.** The prompt asks for "the Taipei time you finish", and the agent
   wrote 04:45 on all three scheduled mornings, after the edition was published (04:33 to 04:38). On 13 Nov it
   wrote 10:12 against a publish at 09:56. Neither the site nor the chat edition shows it, and DATE_MISMATCH
   checks only that it falls between the window's end and the deadline. Calibration can have publish stamp it.
3. **The CVE List's hourly release.** On 16 Nov the zips read at 04:31 (20:31 UTC) ended at 19:00 UTC,
   before the record window's end at 20:00 UTC. The tag lookup worked (`logs/2026-11-16.collect.log` has no
   "tags not listed" line), so the newest hourly release listed at 20:31 UTC was 19:00 UTC's: the 20:00 UTC
   release was more than 30 minutes late. So `cvelist` was stale, the edition partial and the pack one hour of
   new records short (12 records read). The Gap counts to the cutoff two days earlier (14 Nov), so it was
   unaffected. If this recurs, calibration can list the tags again just before drafting and read a release
   that has come out since.
4. **The first week in review (16 Nov).** The text kept to the metrics and said what is not measurable. Its
   `strongest_against` was the FAA ground stop, blamed on an equipment upgrade "with zero evidence of
   anything AI-related". That is not evidence against the thesis. It passes WEEKLY_FLAG, which needs only
   a tier 1 or 2 source. Calibration can tighten the prompt's wording for it.
5. **Link check.** From 15 Nov the post-publish check reports the Microsoft Security blog page cited by
   13 Nov's `ai-linux-kernel-microsoft-2026-11` as `changed` (HTTP 200, content hash different). It edits
   nothing, so this is for a reader to look at, not a failure.
6. **A quiet weekend.** On 15 and 16 Nov the run ranked 110 and 31 candidates, against 312 to 444 on the
   weekdays, and published 4 and 3 items.
7. **Usage.** The seven-day utilization at the scheduled runs was 1% to 12% (46% on 13 Nov, before the
   window reset).
