strings in 16 records, 12 distinct addresses (11 written plainly, 1 spelt out as "nameATdomainDOTcom").

accepted decision covered researchers' names, not addresses. Matching never used them: the finders'
first strip rule removes every token with an @ before the patterns run.

- **Window (26 weeks, 2026-05-11 to 2026-11-08):** 157 flaws disclosed in the window were added to
  CISA's exploited list; 58 of them (37%, 80% range 32% to 42%) were already exploited when disclosed,
  against 37% in 2023 and 2024: no clear change. For the other 99, CISA confirmed exploitation after a
  median 17 days (13 to 22), against 21 in 2023 and 2024: no clear change. That is 2.2 times the 2023 to
  2024 pace of such listings (all CISA listings: 1.9 times).

  13-week mean (5.1 times without the Linux kernel's 4,816), and 3.7 times the same 13 weeks a year
  earlier (12,244).
- **AI-credited (13 weeks):** 502 records whose credits name an AI finder, 224 of them not Anthropic's (a
  lower bound), plus 13 CVE ids that only Anthropic's disclosure ledger lists. One AI-credited flaw since
  2025 is on CISA's list (`CVE-2026-111497`, credited to OpenAI's Codex Security). The base rate: of 289
  AI-credited records published from 2025-01-01 to 2026-08-10, none reached CISA's list within 90 days,
  where the all-records rate (282 of 96,474) would expect 0.73: too few to give a ratio.
- **Fix side:** Anthropic's dashboard (revision 40, as of 2026-11-02): 6,009 reviewed by external
  security firms, 5,087 disclosed, 629 patched upstream. GitHub's reviewed advisories without a fixed
